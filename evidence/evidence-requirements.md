# AI Vendor Evidence Requirements

## Security
ISO/IEC 27001; SOC 2 Type II; penetration testing; vulnerability management; encryption; IAM/MFA; incident response.

## Privacy
DPA; data inventory; retention; deletion evidence; processing locations; transfer safeguards; subprocessor list.

## AI Governance
AI policy; AI risk framework; AI inventory; impact assessment; human oversight; AI incidents; model-change process.

## AI Assurance
Model/system card; training-data governance; performance evaluation; fairness testing; explainability; monitoring.

## Resilience
BCP; DR plan/test; RTO/RPO; exit plan.

## Contract
Data-use restrictions; incident notification; audit rights; model-change notification; subprocessor controls; deletion.
